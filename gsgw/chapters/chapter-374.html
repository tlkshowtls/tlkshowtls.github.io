CHAPTER 374

Daydream Inc.

To be dumped into this lousy company twice.

“Save me.”

It’s not even the headquarters where I used to work.

The Namhae branch...?

What is that? 

There isn’t even an entry for it on the wiki!

I’m certain that after returning home, I recalled things I hadn’t remembered from the wiki.

Even though I’d distanced myself from <Darkness Exploration Records>, there was no doubt about it.

I’m talking about wiki entries, like Sekwang Special City or departments within the Supernatural Disaster Management Agency, that I’d suddenly been unable to recall.

‘Maybe back then, I gradually forgot them as I became absorbed in and submerged by the ghost story’s worldview...’ I even speculated about it.

…Or maybe it was because of the situation I was in regarding the “Good Friend.”

Whichever it was, it left a strange, uneasy feeling, but anyway, it means I now remember the wiki entries I’ve read without any sense of dissonance.

But the Namhae Branch Office?

‘What’s that? I don’t know…’

Of course, I knew Daydream Inc. had branch offices.

…It was more of an “Oh, so that’s how it’s set up” kind of feeling.




Daydream Inc. consists of a headquarters in Seoul, regional branch offices throughout the country, and several specialized overseas branches...




Wouldn’t it be strange for such a large pharmaceutical company to have only a headquarters and no branch offices?

It’s even stranger that ghost stories only happen near Seoul.

So this was more of a setting designed to create natural plausibility.

But then again.

‘…They didn’t actually decide on the specifics for each branch office.’

If they went into that much detail, it wouldn’t be a ghost story’s wiki; it’d turn into an investigative report exposing the company….

After all, this was a wiki created solely for writing ghost stories, and for Daydream Inc., collecting Dream Essence was what mattered most.

Even so, actually seeing a branch office like this was kind of fascinating...




Daydream Corporation

Namhae Branch

Roundoff Squad




The Roundoff Squad?

The Roundoff Squad that I knew?




— They’re just cannon fodder. Think of them as an extra life given to employees who can get the job done; don’t worry about it.




Why is the name of a team used as both slaves and a suicide squad coming up here…? Don’t you get that distinct, chilling feeling that something’s wrong?

And besides…

‘…Isn’t the environment here way too different from headquarters?’

For the record, I was still lying face-down in front of the old ancestral altar.

This was, after all, just a thought that crossed my mind for nearly six seconds after seeing the nameplate that read “Daydream, Inc. – Namhae Branch.”

With my head soaked through and my body completely drained of strength.

This is driving me crazy.

“Huh.”

What on earth is wrong with my body?

I barely managed to lift myself up, almost letting out a whimper (though it probably sounded more like the whine of a mosquito).

As I did, the pig’s head, rice cakes, and fruit on the ancestral altar came into full view.

...They strangely caught my eye.

“......”

‘Am I... extremely hungry right now?’

It suddenly occurred to me that perhaps my body was so weak because I was incredibly hungry…

‘The ancestral altar… If I mess with that at a ghost story company set in a horror universe, it’d be suicide.’

It was a realistic judgment, more than just a sense of foreboding.

With trembling legs, I barely managed to stagger out the door, leaving behind my plate piled high with dried-up food.

‘I'm going to die. Seriously…’

Beyond the door lay an old hallway.

It was completely sealed off and had a strange, underground tunnel-like atmosphere.

‘It’s kind of like an abandoned mine from the 80’s…’



— Religious activity time is about to end. Workers, please check your work quotas for this week.

— When you’re in a pinch, Dream of Money, a Dream Life!

And what on earth is this announcement supposed to mean?

‘Workers? Quotas?’

It felt similar to what I used to hear when I was on the security team, but it also strangely sounded like an advertisement and lacked sincerity. How should I put it...

‘It’s like a moneylending operation.’

I mean, loan sharks.

‘Oh…’

Then, at the far end of the long, winding hallway, I spotted a line of people shuffling along. Maybe one of them was the person who’d poured water over my head.

‘Is that the way?’

I instinctively followed them, but kept a slight distance. Fortunately, the few who glanced back at me seemed uninterested and went right back to talking among themselves.

Everyone was dressed the same.

‘Short-sleeved T-shirts and elastic-waisted pants…’

The ochre color looked like a prison uniform or indoor attire for some kind of group retreat.

And there was one thing that stood out in particular.

‘...A sticker?’

Without exception, they all had a red sticker stuck somewhere on their bodies.

‘What does it say?’

I squinted and could barely make it out.




Y672


Category: Worker

Remaining Deadline: 84 Days



“......”

Could it be that they classify employees that way here?

I looked down at myself. I ran my fingers over my identical earth-colored outfit and finally felt the texture of a sticker attached to my neck.

I had one too.

‘...Can I peel it off?’

Just as I was about to grab the corner to check...

[Hey, hey, that worker over there!]

“...!”

[Worker No. Z999, what you were just about to do is a violation of work regulations. Stop immediately.]

…It was a human voice.

It came from the speaker in the corner of the hallway, the same one that was broadcasting announcements just a moment ago. I carefully pulled my hand away from the sticker and struggled to speak. 

“I didn’t… tear it off. I just touched it.”

[Yes, yes. If you’re caught again, you’ll receive penalty points. Please proceed to the designated break room.]

‘Are they monitoring us?’

The businesslike voice, clearly annoyed, cut off abruptly. But there was nothing suspicious about it—it was just too ordinary. It didn’t feel like someone from the security team.

“...Hmm,”

At least I’d heard my destination.

‘They told me to go to the designated break room.’

I started walking again, and as I followed the crowd, the interior scenery soon changed slightly.

It was a passageway that brought to mind the view of an old, corridor-style apartment building.

— The break area is closing. Good luck this week! Get rich!

Thud.

The moment I entered that section of the hallway, a fire door slammed shut behind me.

I heard the sound of an electronic door lock clicking shut.

‘Locked in…?’

“F*ck....”

“I f*cking hate working.” 

However, as if they were used to this, each worker scanned the stickers in front of the doors in the hallway and entered the rooms one by one.

I looked at the door through which the people had already gone.

A nameplate was attached to the steel door, which had small barred windows like those in a prison.



Y 210~215



I saw similar numbers on the red stickers the people were wearing earlier.

“And the number that called me earlier was…”

— Worker No. Z999.

…Okay.

Still feeling like a beached manta ray, I shuffled forward, dragging my feet. After walking down the hallway for quite a while, I spotted a nameplate on the very last room.



Z 990 ~ 999



With a short beep, the door opened. And I was left suffocated by the horrific conditions inside.

‘Wow.’

A tiny space, not even the size of a single room…

A floor with exposed concrete, cobwebs and mold, and on one side, even a bare mud wall. A foul-smelling pit that probably served as a toilet.

And scattered here and there were a few thin, cheap mats with their stuffing all bursting out.

...That’s it.

Forget a refrigerator; there wasn’t even a chair.

‘What is this?’

Even a prison cell from the 1980’s wouldn’t be this bad.

Plus, if the 990~999 on the nameplate means this room houses workers with those numbers...

“This place is supposed to hold 10 people?”

It’s not like it was some old prisoner-of-war camp or anything.

How was this supposed to be a break room?

It’s more like an abandoned prison....

With a queasy look, I scanned the shabby, chaotic interior once more. And the moment my gaze landed on a single mattress in the dark corner of the room...

I noticed a silhouette.

“Huh?”

Someone was lying on the mattress.

It was a corpse.

“…!!”

I almost bolted out of the room, but I was too weak and ended up just stumbling.

‘Crazy.’

What the hell is this?

I wanted to scream and cry, but I had no choice but to assess the situation. I screamed inwardly as I examined the corpse....

‘Huh.’

The person lying there, staring wide-eyed into space, was a man who looked to be about my age, wearing ochre-colored clothes like all the other workers.

Something that looked like a chocolate wrapper was sticking out of his pocket. Up to that point, it could have been considered somewhat ordinary, but…

‘H-his condition…’

Black, ash-like fluid had oozed from every orifice of his face and dried into a crust, and his left hand was clutching at his heart.

It looked like he’d died of a heart attack.

“Oh, please…”

This was no ordinary sight. To anyone, this would look like a death straight out of a horror story.

I forced myself to read the 'Classification: Worker, Z993' written on the sticker attached to that person's exposed ankle.

It seems he was one of the nine roommates I had in this body…

“Ah…”

This is driving me crazy.

And what’s driving me even crazier is that my body is starting to give out.

‘...I feel dizzy.’

Now I know for sure.

I felt like I was going to lose my mind from hunger.

...And my eyes drifted towards the corpse.

“……Hah.”

I really wanted to scream, but I had no other choice.

I rummaged through the pockets of the corpse.

‘Ugh’

A chocolate bar wrapped in foil, some candy, and... a notebook.

I couldn’t hold back any longer, so I tore open the chocolate wrapper and popped it into my mouth.

“Phew.”

This is insane.

The sugar rush raced from my stomach all the way to my head.

Everything became eerily clear and crisp. My body, suddenly reenergized, took huge bites of the chocolate and moved quickly.

‘Information’

I opened the notebook the corpse had been carrying.




5/5 (probably)

I think I saw a poster offering money in a daydream and contacted them? I’m not sure. Anyway, when I woke up, I was here.

They said I could make 50 million won just by hanging in there for a month, so I started working right away.

They said I couldn’t contact the outside world while working due to security issues, but in return, if I lasted a total of 100 days, I’d get 500 million won. Seriously? Who could stand that? lol

It seems they roughly divide workers into tiers, X, Y, and Z... I ended up in X. Judging by the room, it looks like the better ones are assigned in ascending alphabetical order.

Room Z is no place for humans to live, lol. I guess since it’s a big corporation, their level of “motivation” is on another level.




“It’s the place I’m in right now, you know…”

After that, the post was full of complaints about the facilities and daily life at the Namhae branch of Daydream Inc.

He even compared it to a deep-sea fishing vessel and expressed frustration at being cut off from the outside world.

'Hmm.'

I flipped to the next page.




5/7

A few days into scouting the place.

It seems like everyone here, except for me, is either a deadbeat, a gambler, or a drug addict... wow. My eyes are hurting.

It feels like they’re gathering a bunch of people at rock bottom and making them do dangerous work, but let’s just earn some money fast, get out of here, and be done with it.

So far, they’ve had me set the wooden doll on fire with a lighter every day. It wasn’t a big deal. It burned well. I don’t even know why they’re paying me a few hundred for this crap.

Maybe they’re investigating paranormal activity? Hahaha. I wonder if they’ve incurred a ghost’s grudge while making drugs. I don’t believe in ghosts, so whatever.




“......”

I can see the future coming...

Sure enough, at some point, his handwriting in the diary started to look frantic, and strange phrases began appearing.




5/10

 The wooden doll wouldn’t burn for some reason.

 I poured oil on it and forced it to burn, but after that, I had a weird dream tonight.

 It was like a faceless ghost, cackling as it grabbed my ankle, flipped me upside down, and…

 And dipped me in the water before pulling me out... I don’t know. I was sleep-paralyzed, and I felt like shit.

 I asked if I could quit, but they said if I don’t finish the 30 days, they’ll pay me the minimum wage, so…

 I’m staying for now.

 Must be my imagination. I had a beer at the snack bar today; it’s fucking expensive.




5/12

I couldn't sleep.




5/14

I didn't sleep again today.




5/16

I told them I was quitting because I just couldn’t take it anymore, but the manager kept dodging the issue, putting it off with “later, later,” and making me keep working.

He kept changing his story too. First saying I had to finish the 30 days, then saying I couldn’t quit before that. What the hell is this?




5/16??

Please don’t make me do that wooden doll thing again. Give me something else, please.




“......”




5/35472

They’re making me burn wooden dolls again.

I said I can’t do it and I won’t do it, so my number changed. I’m in Room Z, the last one, but I still have to do the wooden doll work.

I can’t do it. I’m not going out there.




“......”

And on the last page.




I’m sorry, please save me. I’m sorry, please save me. I’m sorry, please save me. I’m sorry, please save me. I’m sorry, please save me. I’m sorry, please save me. I’m sorry, please save me. I’m sorry, please save me.

I’m sorry, please save me.




Thud.

I closed my notebook.

Cold sweat pooled between my fingers.

“……Huh.”

This, to anyone who sees it, is the record of a civilian who died after being repeatedly sent into a ghost story without knowing anything about it.

‘Is this what they do… at this Daydream Inc. branch?’

Did they lure people, those who ‘can be disposed of without repercussions,’ with high pay and then recklessly throw them into ghost stories?

And they didn’t even let them quit?

‘Was there… a setting like this?’

Even when I tried to recall if there had been any mention of this regarding the branch office, there wasn’t anything specific, but I did feel a strange sense of déjà vu.

If I had to describe that feeling…

‘Come to think of it, the Daydream Inc. from the early days of <Darkness Exploration Records> did have this vibe.’

I’m talking about the Daydream Inc. from back when J3, the Security Team Leader, was the Leader of the B Squad.

It has that same flavor from the days when employees were torn apart mercilessly by monsters, given nothing but their bare hands, without a proper set of items or equipment.

Back then, that was the norm…

……

‘Wait a minute.’

“The time period… doesn’t add up.”

Yeah, it was strange.

Just because something was written in the early days of the <Darkness Exploration Records> wiki doesn’t necessarily mean it’s a story set in the past.

In the case of the Supernatural Disaster Management Agency, that’s somewhat true due to the turnover of agents, but Daydream Inc. is different.

‘To begin with, many of the early Daydream Inc. ghost stories were set in the 2020s.’

That couldn’t have been a story from the time when “Wolf,” the team leader of B Squad, was still around.

If that’s the case, then those nameless, numbered Daydream Inc. employees in the ghost stories who were dying off one after another like disposable goods without any safety measures…

‘They were from the branch office.’

Unlike headquarters, the branch office had retained the original flavor of Daydream Inc…!

‘While the headquarters was hiring permanent staff, creating manuals, and securing ‘Dream Essences,’ was this place running like a factory, churning through people for cheap labor…?’

I’m speechless at the sheer plausibility of it all.

I feel like I’m going crazy.

What kind of capitalist horror story is this……



— Headcount complete.



“...!!”

The announcement over the speaker changed.



— Lights out.

— When you’re in a pinch, Dream of Money, a Dream Life!



And the lights in the hallway went out.

“......”

Headcount complete?

This room is currently at ‘1 corpse, 1 human,’ right?

‘Then what about the other eight people….’

Could they all already be dead?

Am I the only survivor in this room?

‘This is a pipe dream, you crazy bastards.’

What the hell is this insane survival rate?

Is this what the Roundoff Squad is all about?

‘I never thought the day would come when I’d long for the days I was a full-time employee at headquarters.’

Even that fatal flaw of not being able to relax because of my roommate was heaven compared to this.

At least back there, my roommate was still alive…

And at least I had a place to sleep in a state-of-the-art officetel near the station...!

“Ugh…”

I staggered to my feet.

Then, using the darkened hallway outside as a mirror, I checked my reflection through the glass slats of the window bars.

That haggard-looking figure… it seemed to be me, but it was too dark to tell for sure.

‘No…’

I got my Church of the Luminous Unknown ID card in my dream to begin with, so why did I wake up at the Daydream Inc. branch?

‘Of course, I’d rather die than wake up in the Church of the Luminous Unknown…’

The words were on the tip of my tongue, but since the chocolate’s thrilling blood sugar spike was already wearing off, I just let out a sigh.

Then I shone the light on the sticker around my neck.
