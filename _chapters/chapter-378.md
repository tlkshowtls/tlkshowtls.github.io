# **CHAPTER 378**

I thought I must have seen it wrong.

But no matter how many times I looked again, nothing changed.

The “crazy person” still in the Y Team dorm—Y491—was… Assistant Manager Jin Nasol.

She was lying on the floor in a complete mess, wearing shabby work clothes.

‘What on earth happened?’

I instinctively crouched down and began to examine her condition more closely.

‘……It looks worse the more I look at her.’

The more I looked, the more I couldn’t help but groan.

I recalled Assistant Manager Jin Nasol from when I was a new hire.

When I recall her once immaculate appearance—as if not a single drop of blood could mar it—the sight of her now, with her hair a complete mess, looking like a wreck, was almost terrifying.

Her hands were covered in scars and calluses. Her nails were all broken.

‘Where her specialized gear used to be…’

The spots where those perfectly manicured, dazzling nail parts—endowed with powerful abilities— had once been were now a gruesome sight. The stark contrast left me speechless.

And… her eyes.

‘…They’re unfocused.’

Assistant Manager Jin Nasol looked as if she had lost her mind, or as if she were possessed.

Even the sight of her lying there, muttering something under her breath like a person who had lost her soul, was unsettling.

“…...”

Why on earth was an assistant manager from headquarters’ elite team in this state, right here?

A place like this, where they lure in debtors and gamblers to make them work?

‘It’s strange to even call this ‘contamination’…’

Even when I recalled the entries featuring Jin Nasol in \<Darkness Exploration Records\>, her fate was never specifically described.

But this wasn’t the kind of place someone would end up drifting into after being left to rot in a contaminated state, was it? It would make more sense if she’d gone to the security team or gone missing in a Darkness.

‘That’s strange.’

I pondered.

And I decided to cautiously try the most intuitive approach.

Lowering my voice so as not to be eavesdropped on, I whispered very softly.

……

“Team A’s Assistant Manager Butterfly.”

At that moment.

Assistant Manager Jin Nasol’s eyes flashed.

“……\!”

Her eyes, narrowed just as they had back then, twitched rapidly as if trying to assess her situation, then she whipped her head around to look at me.

Then her mouth opened, and a rough, hoarse voice came out.

“…Roe Deer?”

“…\!”

She recognized me.

“You… why are you here?”

That’s exactly what I wanted to ask.

“A-are you okay?”

“Does this look okay to you?”

Assistant Manager Jin Nasol’s voice didn’t carry the same piercing irritation as before, but it certainly didn’t sound friendly.

Her voice sounded empty.

‘Still, she does recognize me…’

If there was one silver lining in this bewildering situation, it was that I didn’t appear to be inhabiting someone else’s body—I looked exactly like Kim Soleum.

It seemed my identity and appearance remained unchanged this time as well.

And while seeing the face of someone she knew certainly seemed to help clear Assistant Manager Jin Nasol’s mind…

“Um, could you please sit up first….”

“……”

Jin Nasol’s reaction subsided.

The sharp glint in her eyes faded.

And then.

“Painkillers.”

“Huh?”

“I asked if you have any painkillers.”

A detached voice speaks up.

‘Painkillers?’

“Uh, I don’t have any… but if you go to the infirmary, they might give you some…”

“Shut up.”

“……”

Could it be that she’s… in pain right now?

After hesitating for a moment, I pulled out one of the remaining items I’d brought with me.

“Um, um… what about this?”

A can of beer.

Alcohol can sometimes act as a very primitive painkiller.

I’d even deliberately chosen something with a higher alcohol content so it would serve as “alcohol.” I held it out in front of Assistant Manager Jin Nasol.

“What do you think...”

Click.

One hand snatched the beer away with frightening brutality.

“…\!”

The other hand popped the can open and poured the beer into her mouth as if shoving it right in.

Gulp.

Assistant Manager Jin Nasol’s fingers, gripping the beer, crumpled the aluminum can so tightly that it looked as if it might puncture it.

Gulp, gulp…

“……”

Phew.

The can fell to the floor from Assistant Manager Jin Nasol’s hand as she gasped for breath.

Clink.

“I wasn’t planning on drinking.”

She sat up.

Her eyes were still slightly glazed over, but her voice was much calmer than it had been moments ago when she’d seemed out of her mind.

She says.

“It’s a little better.”

I almost instinctively stepped back.

But I barely managed to stop myself.

“W-Wow, that’s a relief…”

When I deliberately answered in a slightly stammering tone, the other person’s expression darkened.

“Why do you talk like that…?”

But soon, a sharp, mocking sneer curled at the corners of Assistant Manager Jin Nasol’s mouth.

“I guess I shouldn’t be the one saying that, looking like this.”

“……”

I shook my head slightly.

“Um… I don’t know who you are, but if you don’t mind...”

“Nobody’s eavesdropping.”

……\!

“I, I don’t understand what you mean...”

“Like a damn cockroach... if they kept the bugs in every single person's room running all the time, it'd waste too much data. They switch them on and off. Right now, it's turned off." 

Like a rusted clockwork mechanism slowly grinding back to life, Jin Nasol's voice, which had come out sluggish and uneven, gradually shed its roughness and returned to its usual calm, rational tone. 

At last, pressing a hand against her forehead, she jerked her chin toward the door. 

"Over there. By the door. When it's on, it makes this annoying high-pitched beeping sound." 

…I guess that means if they turn on the listening device, she’ll hear a high-pitched mechanical noise, like an ultrasonic sound.

“Huh? Oh, so… it’s not making a ‘beep’ sound now…?”

“Yeah. Don’t make me say it twice. I’ll get even more annoyed.”

……

“Understood.”

“…\!”

I replied in a manner befitting Supervisor Kim Soleum.

“Even if the listening device is off, the CCTV cameras monitoring the workers are surely still running, so I’ll continue this conversation as is.”

I looked at her.

Surprisingly, Jin Nasol averted her gaze, looking off to the side.

“Assistant Manager. When I came to, I suddenly found myself hired as a worker here. I’ve been trying to figure out what’s going on, and now I see you’re here too.”

“……”

“Why are you here?”

“What good would it do for you to know?”

“What on earth happened?”

“I’m asking what you’re going to do with that information.”

“We could help each other out.”

“Ha, coming all the way in here just to…”

The voice, which had been mocking me, suddenly trailed off, and she looked at me.

As if suddenly remembering something.

“You were the top recruit, weren’t you?”

“Yes.”

And I was promoted in the shortest amount of time, too.

“……”

“I came to my senses two days ago.”

I pointed to the sticker on my neck.

Z999.

“I was in this state back then.”

“You……”

“And now I’m number Y492.”

“…\!”

In just two days, I’d gone from the very last number to a number at the front, skipping an entire team.

“……”

I could feel her gaze on me as she sized me up.

Jin Nasol’s eyes sharpened slightly, and she nodded.

“…Okay.”

It seemed to be tacit approval.

Her voice grew calmer, and her demeanor settled.

After a moment, Assistant Manager Jin Nasol began speaking as if giving a briefing.

“But why you’re here is none of my business. My situation has nothing to do with yours.”

“Understood.”

I wasn’t expecting anything anyway.

“What’s your other question?”

“This Namhae branch, why exactly…”

“Why is it full of nothing but this kind of trash?”

A sneer curls at the corner of her mouth.

“Can’t you tell just by looking? This place was built to use these so-called ‘workers’ and then throw them away. If anyone from headquarters wants to earn points fast, they’ll grab them and send them here as managers.”

“…\!”

“That’s what the ‘remote work allowance’ is all about.”

If the managers here earn points quickly…

“So it’s true that the workers have Dream Essence Collectors on their wristbands, after all.”

“You’re still sharp. That’s right. The Dream Essence collected there goes to the person in charge. Though, every time it gets passed up the food chain, the higher-ups will take a cut.”

So it’s a pyramid scheme.

‘That researcher who was doing the announcements—he must’ve earned more than the field investigators…’

“But there’s no monthly salary.”

Wow.

‘So it’s an either-or choice between points and money.’

I grabbed my throbbing head.

Then it hit me.

  —…Kim Soleum-ssi, the new hire, did you happen to receive a business card or something?

It was the business card I’d received from the orientation host after the entrance exam at Daydream.

Kwak Jekang’s voice echoes in my head.

  

   — If you want to earn points faster, contact that person.

  

‘…No way.’

Was that really the business card that led to my assignment to the branch office…\!?

‘It’s downright scary.’

I never would have guessed that the path leading me here had been open all along.

I’d speculated about this once before, but it turned out to be exactly the kind of prediction I’d hoped to avoid...

Of course, since it was now a line of work that treats workers like disposable commodities rather than actual workers, the situation was completely different.

In any case, given the working conditions, the rumor that the branch office was a living hell was entirely plausible.

But...

‘If that’s the case, it’s even stranger that Assistant Manager Jin Nasol is here.’

Shouldn’t she have been promoted to a higher position and be there, replacing workers?

‘Was there something unusual?’

Something like that...

……

‘Wait a minute.’

There was one thing, after all.

“…Could Assistant Manager Jin Nasol being here be related to Director Ho’s disappearance?”

“…\!\!”

Ho Yuwon.

The spirit of Sekwang Special City.

I thought of the Fox Counseling Room, which had been marked as temporarily closed. That counselor, who must be busy with a long schedule of sessions with his clients.

‘But from Daydream Inc’s perspective... Director Ho suddenly vanished that very day, didn’t he?’

And Elite Team A, where Assistant Manager Jin Nasol was assigned... was part of Director Ho’s division\!

So much so that the entire team had been drafted into Director Ho’s project.

“Could it be that after Director Ho’s project disappeared, there was a problem within the company...”

“Shut up.”

“…\!”

“I told you not to ask about things unrelated to your situation. Don’t you understand?”

That was right.

‘Judging by this overreaction, I’ve hit the nail on the head…\!’

I looked at Jin Nasol, who was staring at me with fierce eyes. I braced my feet and stood up straight.

She seemed to have barely regained her composure, so I didn’t provoke her. Since it seemed difficult to dig deeper in this direction, I asked a different question.

Something I’d been wanting to find out for a while.

“So, how much time has passed since Director Ho disappeared?”

“What?”

“I’m asking how long it’s been.”

Ho Yuwon and I must have disappeared around the same time.

How much time has passed here since I disappeared?

Her mouth slowly opened.

……

“Three years.”

Huh?

“Yeah. It’s been about that long.”

Wait a minute.

3 years?

'I spent a year at home…’

In the meantime, several years have passed here.

The back of my head was starting to tingle.

'So now...'

Just how much has the situation changed?

One year and three years feel completely different.

How much time is left until this \<Darkness Exploration Records\> universe falls apart? As for the others’ conditions—could it be that, by any chance………

‘No.’

Even now, it’s not a question with an answer. I shouldn’t get anxious for no reason. I took a deep breath.

‘Anyway, one thing is certain.’

Even in this condition, this person was still the most capable Darkness explorer here.

Looking at my former superior, who had lost all her specialized equipment and even her sense of direction, I finally said,

“I will hold out for 14 days and get out of here.”

“......”

“Like before, I still have the confidence I can do it.”

If there’s one thing this person still has, it must be this:

Experience in exploring darknesses.

“I don’t know what your wish is, Assistant Manager, but I’m sure we can find another way besides the Wish Ticket. After all, Daydream isn’t the only faction that deals with ghost stories.”

This wasn’t just a persuasive argument; it was also the truth.

“How about we aim to get out of here first and then find another way?”

“......”

“I just hope we can work together as a team, at least until we make it out.”

I waited.

Jin Nasol seemed to be staring at the floor, but eventually raised her head.

“Let’s try it.”

The light had returned to her eyes.

\*\*\*

After stepping into the bathroom for a moment, Assistant Manager Jin Nasol emerged looking completely put together.

The wrinkles in her shabby work uniform had been smoothed out, and her appearance was now neat and tidy.

This also meant that the hair that had been hanging loosely below her shoulders was completely gone.

“...\!”

“It was getting in the way.”

Her eyes, visible through the bob cut, trimmed razor-sharp to a length that barely touched her chin, were fierce and sharp.

“What are you looking at?”

“…I was just thinking, you always look neat when you work.”

“Hah.”

Jin Nasol snorted derisively but didn’t get angry.

Beep.

And at that moment, the break room door opened.

“…So why the hell does a single cigarette cost 5,000 won?”

“That’s exactly what I’m saying. This company is pulling all kinds of tricks just to avoid paying us… Huh?”

The people from Team Y, Section 490, were pouring into the room.

And one middle-aged man who had been showing off narrowed his eyes at Jin Nasol and started pointing an accusing finger at her.

“Whoa, what’s going on? That idiotic bitch has come to her senses……”

“Hey.”

Ah.

“Don’t talk. It’s noisy.”

“What, what?”

“Why?”

Jin Nasol, who had been furrowing her brow, strode purposefully toward him.

“Y-you were just muttering to yourself, and now you’re really losing it...”

“Can’t you understand what I said the first time? "

“What……guh, guh……\!”

I watched, horrified, as Jin Nasol choked a Y-team member in the same break room.

That’s right. This person’s personality was always like this...\!

Muscles and veins were visible on her arms beneath her shabby work clothes. She was a mess, but that intensity was still there…   
Ah, no, that’s not it\!

I hurriedly tried to stop her.

“U-um…\! Don’t burn off those calories. It’ll sober you up\! Stop it…\!”

“......”

Thud.

Thankfully, Jin Nasol let the guy from Team Y fall to the floor.

She did toss him, though.

“From now on, keep your mouth shut. Breathe quietly, too. Got it?”

“Uh, uuuuh...”

“If you make a noise, I’ll burn your tongue first.”

She really seemed like a crazy person.

This group of workers, who were all about “tough on the weak, soft on the strong”, kept glancing warily at Jin Nasol’s unusual tone and demeanor, and instead of charging at her, they quietly slipped into their rooms.

They chose to avoid the confrontation for now rather than fight.

...Huh?

'Wait... at this rate, I might actually avoid getting picked on for no reason...?' 

Just in case the people inside the rooms could hear us, I decided to put on one last performance. 

“U-Um... i-it'll hurt if you hit me, so... p-please don't... ”

"That way of talking is getting on my nerves. Knock it off." 

“......”

Yeah.

Anyway, that’s why I decided to take a new stance.

A.K.A., the sidekick to the crazy gangster.

‘Alright.’

This might actually be comfortable...\!

“Um, then should I go get some painkillers from the infirmary...?”

Silence.

Soon, a flat voice replied.

“I don’t have any money.”

“......”

What on earth have you been up to…

“I'll lend you the money...”

“Whatever.”

And so, I head to the infirmary of my own free will…

Yeah.

Earlier, I was doing my best to resist the researcher and refuse to get treated at the infirmary, so I couldn't go. But now I have an excuse to go voluntarily, which was great…

It’s going to look like I got ripped off to anyone who sees it, isn’t it?

‘I should use this chance to get prepared.’

By the way, I didn’t even have to worry about how to find a workaround to make the purchase.

\[Infirmary\]

When I went inside, it turned out to be a self-service area....

All the medications were dispensed from vending machines.

‘You daydream lunatics.....’

Anyway, I bought some painkillers.

I also picked up a few first-aid supplies for myself, and without hesitation, I bought some cliché medical items that are often used as plot triggers or useful tools in horror stories.

The price was horrendous.

‘210,000 won…?\!’

You’d think this was some dystopian country without health insurance. I swallowed hard and handed over the money with trembling hands.

Still, I felt a sense of closure.

Walking down the hallway back to Team Y’s room, I sorted through my thoughts.

‘I’ve moved rooms, and I’ve even made some colleagues of my own.’

What kind of darkness will be unleashed next?

‘I wonder what the researcher would have chosen after the wooden doll.’

For some reason, I had a feeling they might give me something reasonably safe this time. It didn’t seem like a reckless streak of luck; rather, if they wanted to test my luck “steadily,” they’d likely have a long-term plan in place.

‘There’s a high probability I’ll be assigned to a relatively low-risk location this time.’

And I think that was a pretty good guess.

...If only this one variable hadn’t come up.

\[There’s a special night shift today.\]

\[X100 series, Y400 series, Z900 series—all units begin moving to the worksite.\]

“Huh?”

“What’s going on? Why are we all heading to the same place?”

That night.

All of us workers were simultaneously thrown into a single ghost story.
