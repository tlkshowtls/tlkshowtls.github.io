# **CHAPTER 373**

I returned home.

What that sentence on the edited Darkness Exploration Records website was pointing to was...

It was definitely me.

Each and every word was so clear that I couldn’t think of anything else.

“.......”

A chilling sensation crept up my spine.

I immediately closed the screenshot of the edited screen.

Still, that strange feeling wouldn’t go away.

It was like realizing that what I thought was a streetlight’s shadow in a dark alley was actually someone staring at me intently.

“...That,”

Why haven’t other people noticed this?

Surely there must have been people who thought to dissect the website—yet no one extracted that sentence after such a simple edit?

“That’s strange.” It was strange.

—Like a ghost story.

That’s why all of this felt even more chilling—it hit me so directly.

Still, there’s one thing I was grateful for.

The fact that I was surprisingly calm.

‘…Why is that?’

Ah.

‘It’s because I’m used to it.’

The mind that survived Darkness Exploration Records.

The brain that had actually experienced countless ghost stories—after a year of avoiding the truth—had finally stopped evading reality and begun to face the situation head-on, its gears spinning rapidly to process everything.

“…—First, the sentence.” My hand swiftly pulled the screenshot back up.

After you returned home, the apocalypse has come.

Thank you so much,

\[Ireum-nim\].

…The apocalypse.

At first glance, it seems as though my return home was the cause of the apocalypse, but if you read it carefully again, it’s not about cause and effect.

It means the apocalypse came ‘after’ I returned home.

‘It’s just a matter of chronology.’ But…

‘If that’s the case, there’s no need to thank me at the end.’

That’s...

……

.......

“‘Thank you for ignoring that the apocalypse was coming and just going home….’ Is that what it means?”

...Yeah.

I definitely knew it.

\<Darkness Exploration Records\>.

A ghost story wiki compiling the management methods and investigation cases of three distinct factions that collect and manage dangerous paranormal phenomena.

That one prerequisite was necessary to sustain this worldview:

...That paranormal phenomena are rampant enough in the world to warrant collection.

Especially considering the tendency of dopamine-driven internet content to create increasingly sensational and dangerous ghost stories as time goes on…

This world inevitably becomes more and more dangerous and horrific, leading to a single conclusion that satisfies all plausibility.

'...The world will be destroyed due to a failure in containment.'

\- Faced with strange phenomena beyond humanity’s capacity to handle, everyone eventually dies.

A gloomy and chilling ending that fits the genre perfectly.

\[Prophecy of the Apocalypse: Darkness Exploration Records\]

Ultimately, all the ghost stories on the wiki are prophecies of the apocalypse or its precursors.

Creating various bizarre phenomena and props based on that premise is precisely... the worldview of \<Darkness Exploration Records\>.

It was also an extremely convenient setting for creative purposes.

**\- If there were ghost stories like this, humanity would be doomed, lol.**

    **\- Yeah, that’s why it really is screwed.**

“How could a horror story of this magnitude be managed?” 

That’s all it takes to comfortably answer this question.

That they failed to contain it, and everything went to ruin...

“........”

So I knew it, too.

\-I didn’t even need to think three years ahead. Because, after all, this world is…

\<Darkness Exploration Records\> When I opened my eyes in that world,

This was definitely one of the reasons I made my choices by focusing solely on the present, without considering the future.

‘That world won’t last long anyway.’

If I couldn’t go home, there was a high probability I’d die a horrible death anyway...

“.......”

Yeah.

Everyone who helped me get back home will soon die.

The members of Field Exploration Squad D, the Hyunmoo Team 1 operatives, the other field operatives, the sergeants of the elite team, the security team members—all of them...

“...Stop.”

‘Phew.’

I clenched and unclenched my fist.

‘Start by trying to understand the situation.’ The website that went down, the hidden message that seemed to be targeting me. So, if I think about it based on the situation…

“...It seems like they know something about me.” To be precise, it’s about the “Ireum-nim” who visited the world of Darkness Exploration Records.

“…Who is it?”

Let’s find out.

I reconnected to the internet.

‘As far as I remember, the wiki site wasn’t run by a specific individual from the start.’ It naturally formed from a collection of ghost stories that had become popular on social media, and it had been sustained through donations and advertising.

‘But there has to be someone paying for the server fees and maintaining the website address.’ And I found it without much trouble.

It was the social media accounts of the original operators of \<Darkness Exploration Records\>.

Perhaps because of the many inquiries regarding the website’s disappearance, a related post was at the very top of the feed.

**\-We’re getting a lot of frantic inquiries about a certain wiki site being taken down, but I haven’t been involved with it for quite some time. Please look elsewhere.**

They’re saying they’ve already stepped away from operations.

**\-Wait, so if it’s not you, who’s running it now?**

   **\-I don’t know. I quit because I was overwhelmed by real-life issues, but there are probably a few people left.** 

So I looked up all the accounts of those “people left.”

The result:

**\-No, I told you I don’t know anything about it. I already said I had nothing to do with it back when those pop-ups were driving me crazy.**

Fuck.

**\-No inquiries regarding the detector.**

**\-I’m in Germany right now. I don’t run the wiki. I quit the year before last.**

I came to a startling realization.

“It doesn’t exist.”

No one is running the wiki site.

Everyone who had once been part of the Darkness Exploration Records website's admin team had already stepped down.

And that means…

**\-Oh, so they all just happened to have quit before the pop-up event? Hahaha**

**\-How come not a single one of them showed up? Since when was “Darkness Exploration Records” an unmanned online store?**

**\-Damn, that’s so obvious lol \= They’ve got something to hide**

**\-Just how much did they rake in from that pop-up?**

**\-Whoa, this is a total rip-off**

The point is that everyone had already quit before the pop-up store opened.

It did look like they were reaping commercial benefits and then washing their hands of it by claiming no involvement.

So people were whispering, “It’s definitely about the money.”

“It’s a practical decision.”

Of course, it was only natural to suspect that.

But my mind…

I’ve come to a slightly different conclusion.

“.......”

A chilling premonition I feel as someone who has actually experienced supernatural phenomena and urban legends.

‘..What if there really is no administrator at all?’

A wiki site that maintains itself.

What if that in itself… has become a ghost story?

“.......”

What if ghost stories are manifesting in reality here, too?

“No… wait a minute.”

I shouldn’t jump to conclusions and start screaming just yet.

‘If that were really the case, social media would be out of control.’

Rumors would have spread uncontrollably and gone viral. In reality, there’s no Disaster Management Agency—if real ghost stories suddenly started appearing, there’s no way rumors wouldn’t spread.

So, I’m just making a very small-scale assumption. Just an assumption....

\-What if the \<Darkness Exploration Records\> wiki is the only one experiencing these strange phenomena?

And.

…What if it’s a state that only I can perceive?

“......”

I was the only one who realized that phrase seemed to be aimed directly at me.

‘Wait a minute.’

Then, the abandoned pop-up store I see in my dreams—could it be...

...Could it actually be real?’ Goosebumps ran down my spine.

I jumped up from my seat.

“This is driving me crazy.”

I’d been holding on, thinking it was just a nightmare.

‘Could that have led to something even worse?’

Examples of people who ignored ghost stories as “nightmares” or “just their imagination” and ended up in trouble flashed through my mind.

And my brain, having read far too many of them, naturally conjured up a horrific ending.

‘Damn it.’

Cold sweat trickled down my back for the first time in a long while.

At this rate, I’d end up pulling my hair out.

Feeling like I wanted to tear my hair out, I ran my fingers through it.

‘...What should I do?’

And then it hit me.

“Seriously...”

In the end, if I wanted to properly understand this situation, there was only one answer.

Not to ignore it.

“I’ll take the initiative.” If I couldn’t avoid it, I had to deal with it.

Assuming all these phenomena are actually ghost stories, I’ll conduct a proper investigation.

‘And the clues that connect them all are...’

I’d already found it.

“…The pop-up store.”

And I still had evidence of that connection.

One was in my dream.

The other was… in reality.

“.......”

I started to get out of bed.

Now my body was heading toward the entryway.

But instead of opening the door and stepping outside, I bent down

and...

opened the cabinet in the entryway.

Squeak.

When I looked in the bottom compartment of the open cabinet, I saw a familiar shape inside.

“...Phew.”

A black box.

\<Darkness Exploration Records: Real Goods Box\>.

The item I’d picked up at the pop-up store.

It was the very same one I’d used on my way home.

I couldn’t bring myself to throw it away, but I couldn’t bear to leave it out in plain sight either, so I’d stored it away like this.

I took the box out, placed it in the living room, and opened it.

Inside, I could still see the pink rabbit plushie.

I picked it up.

“…Hello.”

It’s funny, but a chuckle spreads across my lips.

My heart is pounding.

It’s not fear—it’s a strange mix of joy and nervousness.

“I really… went through hell.” No, maybe it’s because you were always there with me through all that hell.

“…Let’s give it a try.”

A horror story-style approach.

First, before flying off, I back up the entire contents of the wiki site onto my smartphone.

It’s not hard if I use the search engine’s “cached page” feature.

“With this, I’ve saved \<Darkness Exploration Records\>.”

Next, I think about the device to set up in the bedroom.

It’s simple. I’ll hang a paper cup filled with cold water from the headboard, and secure the string holding the cup to the floor with ice.

That way, as the ice naturally melts, the string will come loose, and the water will pour over my head, waking me up.

It was an emergency escape plan, just in case.

‘Finally…’

This item—no, this black box full of merchandise.

“......”

As I looked at that merchandise, a method I knew naturally came to mind.

A trick used in some high school ghost story from a nightmare.

A way to wake up while still holding a real-world object in my dream.

“......”

I slipped the box of merchandise under my pillow.

It was a huge box—so big it made my neck quite uncomfortable—but I solved the problem by piling more blankets under me to adjust the height.

“…Phew.”

Yeah, that’s right. I used to be this nervous.

I didn’t know if I’d be able to sleep like this, but I really wanted to avoid taking sleeping pills for no reason and then ending up in a situation where I was asking, “Why can’t I wake up?”...

I ended up tossing and turning quite a bit before finally dozing off at dawn.

And then I opened my eyes.

The dark basement of the department store.

At the spot where the pop-up store had been, now demolished.

I looked down and saw the merchandise box in both my hands.

“Ah.”

It actually worked.

‘Is this really a ghost story?’

I don’t know if I should be happy that this turned out to be true or if I should cry...

*Rrrrrrr…*

*Thud.*

“...\!”

I almost jumped out of my skin.

Wait, does that mean that roulette sound was really… a paranormal phenomenon, too?

And I realize once again.

…So you can break out in a cold sweat even in a dream.

‘Phew.’

The problem is, there's no one here to talk this over with.

After some hesitation, I opened the box, took out the rabbit plushie, and put it in the pocket of my pajamas.

The merchandise didn’t react in that mysterious way, as if they were actual items like before, but even this alone was strangely comforting.

‘...Let’s go.’

*Rrrrrrr…*

*Thud.*

I clutched the rabbit plushie once more, then let it go, and slowly began to walk.

“I’m glad I brought a few extra things to put in the merchandise box.”

Click—I turned on the flashlight I’d brought.

A fairly bright light illuminated the pop-up store site, now an empty lot. Scraps of talismans, traces of dark red “blood” effects, discarded wrapping paper, and a metal shelf rustled beneath my feet.

Uuuugh.

“I totally forgot that things that are only vaguely visible are even scarier…\!”

I’d never even been to a horror escape room since I moved back home…\!

My heart, which had grown complacent from nearly a year of routine, was screaming, but I didn’t stop walking.

Thud.

And with every step I took…

The sound grew closer and closer.

*Rrrrrrr…*

*Thud.*

Finally, I reached the very vicinity of the sound.

…Beyond the shelf.

***Rrrrrrr…***

***Thud.***

“......”

What is it?

“Huh.”

Near where the counter used to be at the pop-up store.

A flashlight… Is that one? No, it’s already too late.

I may as well just get a clear view.

I barely managed to steady my hands, which were drenched in cold sweat.

Then, I peeked my head slightly around the shelf to check what was there...

“......\!"

I saw the roulette wheel.

…But it didn’t move.

“Huh?”

The roulette wheel just sat there, motionless.

The sound continued.

***Rrrrrrr…***

***Thud.***

“…Wait a second.”

A spark went off in my head.

That wasn’t the sound of a roulette wheel.

I had been imagining what the sound was based on my own assumptions—the roulette wheel at the pop-up store where I’d unconsciously won a merchandise box.

But… I was wrong.

“The sound of a roulette wheel spinning isn’t loud enough to be heard from several meters away.” If that’s the case…

‘…..\!'

I quickly swiveled the flashlight to the side.

In the darkness, the true nature of another object was revealed.

***Rrrrrrr…***

***Thud.***

The day I fell into the world of \<Darkness Exploration Records\>.

Actually, I didn’t just use the roulette wheel.
