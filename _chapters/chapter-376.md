# **CHAPTER 376**

*Thud.*

With a short sound, the area around the wooden doll’s shoulder shook. A creaking sound came from the wood upon contact with my hand.  
Naturally, it was my fist that had struck the doll.

*Drip*.

My cold sweat dripped onto the wooden doll.

\[Again.\]

*Thud*.

The doll’s joints twisted, and its arm turned at a strange angle.

The wooden doll had no facial features.

So, even though it couldn’t actually look at me, under the eerily red lighting, I felt that the smooth face of the shadowed sculpture was staring blankly at me. And then...

The insidious voice of an announcement.

\[Yes, yes. Keep going until it breaks.\]

..........

“I don’t want to.”

To be honest. 

‘Of course, as soon as I read the diary entry, I figured out what kind of ghost story it was.’

I went through every ghost story about “wooden dolls” recorded in the \[Darkness Exploration Record\] in my head. 

I narrowed it down to two or three. At that moment, I figured out what it was.

“I said I don’t want to, okay?”

I spoke again without looking up. 

Silence filled the room. 

And then.

\[.....\]

The researcher remained silent.

‘Just as I thought.’

But just as I started to step back, that indifferent voice spoke again.

\[Ah\~ that’s not enough. Let’s keep going.\]

They only reacted to my movements. 

‘They can’t hear my voice.’

I had been almost certain ever since my question overlapped with the researcher's voice earlier. 

There was no doubt they’d installed a speaker inside the isolation room just to issue one-sided orders.

'I've seen this setup several times in the exploration logs.' 

Records written based solely on CCTV footage.

   **The workers who entered Qterw-C-348's containment chamber complained that it was too dark (inferred from infrared camera footage). The researcher repeatedly instructed them to remain where they were.** 

It made sense for the wiki to present information that way, since the limited format heightened the readers' sense of tension. But why would they actually do it like this? 

It was simple.

‘It’s a ghost story where sound plays a role.’

Right, so the researcher determined that auditory elements inside the containment room could serve as a contamination trigger.

'The wooden doll. The ghost in the dream. The waterside. And now... the risk of contamination through sound.' 

Once those keywords are in place, it’s easy to pinpoint a specific ghost story from this fragmentary situation, even without a single hint from the manual.

‘So…’

This was it.

\================

**Darkness Exploration Records / Ghost Stories**

**\[Let's wrap it up here.\]**

**A ghost story appearing in \<Darkness Exploration Records\>. The identification code from Daydream Inc. is Qterw-C-2554.**

**This entity was found buried beneath the soil near the water's edge.**

**Its size ranges from 12 cm to 37 cm, and its materials vary, using cotton, wood, brass, parchment, ■■, and others. They all have a human form consisting of two hands and two legs. Each instance can be identified as Qterw-C-2554 by the talisman attached to it.**

**The talisman was severely damaged and covered in markings. Upon restoring and interpreting the inscription, it was confirmed that it contained phrases used in a Sumang-gut**¹ **ritual for a drowning victim.**

**(Although the horoscope of a drowned person with an erased name is written, no matching deceased person record can be found.)**

**Anyone who comes into contact with the doll begins to experience hallucinations—such as seeing ghosts, being near water, or drowning—starting that very day, and if the condition is left untreated, they will die within seven days.**

**In cases other than suicide, the autopsy results indicate drowning as the cause of death.**

\================

‘I've stumbled upon something truly creepy…’

Phew*.*

Anyway, the wooden doll wasn’t important from the start. It’s just one of the forms this ghost story takes.

And burning or destroying the doll wasn’t the decisive factor either.

‘The ghost story has already begun just by coming into contact with it.’

In other words, that meant… 

*Thud*.

‘Even if I break this, things won't get any worse...\!’

I swung my arm and kept striking the wooden doll.

‘Uuuugh…’

It was the kind of feeling you’d get when you deliberately cut the rope blocking the entrance to a mountain shrine marked “No Trespassing”.

An instinctive sense that I was doing something wrong reverberated through my whole body, but I didn’t stop.

*Thud*.

Just as the researcher had demanded, I kept striking it until it was completely shattered. Even in this body, in a state bordering on malnutrition and screaming in pain, I still had enough strength as a grown man to dislocate the wooden doll’s joints.

And so, the ominous wooden doll in the isolation room was completely...

Smashed to pieces under my grasp.

\[Yes. That’s it.\]

I let go of it at the researcher’s voice.

My knuckles were throbbing, but that wasn’t what mattered. 

What mattered was what came next.

‘Now...\!’

I immediately ducked my head down under the chair and began digging into the exposed dirt floor of the isolation room beneath it.

It had already been dug up quite a bit.

That’s because while I was striking the wooden doll, I’d been skillfully scraping at the floor with my foot the whole time…\!

\[Time to check out... Z999?\]

It’s so we can wrap this up quickly before the authorities show up.

In one swift motion, I scooped up the dirt from the already dug-up spot with both hands.

Then I picked up the doll—its joints all dislocated and broken—that was lying on the chair... placed it in the hole, and covered it back up with dirt.

\[No....\]

Just as I patted down the soil and stood up.

*Ha*

“......”

*I-i-na-ta-ae-jo-eun-mi-seu-geu-ri-pa-do-i-e* ²

A strange, crackling sound—like rewinding a tape—fleetingly reached my ears from the isolation room.

A voice whispering right next to me, so close I could almost hear their breath.

‘Shit.’

Goosebumps prickled the back of my neck, but I shut my eyes tight and pushed the sensation away. 

*Click.*

The door to the isolation room opened behind me.

‘Save me.’

I fled the dark isolation room as if running for my life.

As soon as I stepped outside—under the somewhat coercive watch of a security guard who was already waiting there—I heard the researcher’s voice.

\[Hey, what were you just doing in there?\]

His voice sounded somewhat intrigued.

‘I’ve got an excuse ready for moments like this.’

I deliberately hunched my shoulders. Then, in the same subdued tone I’d used that morning, I said,

“Well, if it’s broken… I have to clean it up, right? So, I cleaned it up… out of sight…”

As if I’d lost my mind a little due to malnutrition and poor living conditions.

\[…Hmm.\]

I made it through.

‘Thanks, you piece-of-crap environment…’

The fact that I can avoid reasonable suspicion even like this—that’s the only upside...

And as I staggered forward, a security guard tried to remove the armband that was fastened around my arm.

\[Wait a minute. Leave that on. Let’s see how things go until tomorrow.\]

‘That’s right.’

You want to record this unusual case too, don’t you? I knew you’d make that kind of choice—just like a researcher at Daydream’s laboratory...

I thought of some crazy researchers back at headquarters.

I felt like I couldn’t laugh or cry. 

It was no different being a branch manager, I guess.

Ah, the only difference is the financial pressure.

\[If that band gets damaged, they’ll deduct the entire cost from your salary. It’s incredibly expensive. Got it? Unless you’re willing to work for years without pay, treat it as if it’s more precious than your own body.\]

“Yes…”

They were even spouting crazy office nonsense like they were demanding I would pay for a computer that broke while an employee was using it.

\[Did you feel anything strange when you were looking at the doll inside, or did you hear anything coming from inside?\]

Hmm.

I shook my head.

\[Hmm… Understood.\]

So I got back on the mine cart and waited a little while for the other workers to finish their shifts and return.

Phew.

'Well... I passed the first round.'

I finally let out a sigh of relief.

I stared at the armband still wrapped around my forearm and fell deep in thought.

I carefully raised my hand, pretended to promise myself I’d take good care of the armband, and gently ran my fingers over it to check the outline inside.

...I could feel a small, cylindrical object.

‘Just as I thought.’

It looked like it contained a dream essence collector.

‘They’ve placed the collector inside the wristband. So the supervisor collects all the solutions that the workers extract from the ghost stories.’

I suspected this armband had functions beyond mere tracking and recording.

After all, the whole point of this operation was to collect dream essence.

The workers are in a position—akin to contract laborers—where they don’t even need to share that information...

But the fact that I now have a dream essence collector in my hand means…

‘...I can give the researchers a real surprise with the grade of this solution.’

My physical condition is already so poor that if I kept staying in this room—which doesn’t even feel like a break room—I’d probably get sick within two weeks…

Hmm…

‘As I thought, I’ll have to move up a few spots.’

Even if I did end up leaving, that was.

I took off my armband and mentally went over the tasks I needed to complete.

\*\*\*

A short while later.

Sitting in the cafeteria, I realized how this absurd working environment for laborers was being sustained.

“Hey, it's pretty good today, too.”

I’d just received my bonus in cash.

Do you know how much it was?

One million won.

‘Crazy.’

I could hear the workers next to me rustling money in their excitement. I, too, almost got carried away for a moment as I looked at the 20 crisp 50,000-won bills.

‘Wow.’

Cash has the power to make you lose your senses.

The numbers displayed on the app screen and the wads of cash in my hand had a completely different impact.

Why else would they use a “money gun” for a Parents’ Day event? Dozens of 50,000-won bills had the power to make people giddy.

It was as if the dopamine reward circuit in their brains had gone into overdrive, much like a gambler chasing the next win.

...So much so that it was hard to notice that some workers hadn’t returned from their morning shifts.

‘Ha…’

“Um, are you going in again this afternoon?”

"Oh, come on. Of course I'm going."

Just so you know, I'm not going in.

— Don’t go this afternoon. Just stay in the garage, move as little as possible, and stay put. It’s best not to do anything unnecessary.

That was what the researcher said.

I was just safely and diligently shoveling the “lunch” in front of me into my mouth.

...At least they gave us a free meal.

But you know what?

‘Wow. They’re even discriminating when it comes to food....’

Even in the company cafeteria, the lower your employee number, the better the meals. The higher your number, the more pitiful your food becomes. 

In other words, I was getting the worst meal of all the workers here.

Even for a ghost-story world, wasn't this crossing the line...? 

‘I want to report this to the Ministry of Labor. ’

For the record, the standard meal provided to the Z900 series workers consisted of nothing more than a lump of cold rice and stir-fried anchovies.

Of course, it seemed like you could order additional items if you paid extra...

Prices are out of control.

\[Tteok Ramyeon – 10,000 won\]

I couldn’t help but think, these people are absolutely insane. 

If the story in the diary of the late Z993 is true, most workers must be in an environment where they were blinded by greed, but if things continued like this, they’d inevitably become reluctant to purchase goods and services out of their own pockets.

So naturally, they’d become obsessed with getting an “early number”.

That allows them to get by without paying.

Wasn’t that a more proactive attitude than just putting up with something a little unsettling?

It’s easy to think that people who go missing are either weak-willed or unlucky and have been taken by ghosts.

‘The psychological manipulation is subtle.’

Since they’re cut off from the outside world, they become even more entrenched in their own little world.

Plus, if a worker ended up making unavoidable expenses, the daydreamers naturally stood to profit by recovering at least a portion of the wages they’d already paid out.

‘Phew.’

I held back a sigh.

For the record, there’s one more reason for this sigh.

“Hey. Is it good?”

"Oh\~ This must be exactly the kind of food our cooing pigeon³ loves. Look at the stir-fried anchovies."

That was because the duo, X141 and X145 who spotted me eating, had come over again and were peering around.

Their purposes seemed clear.

“Our pigeon has to pay back the water from yesterday.”

“......”

There went half my daily pay.

‘Man, you heartless bastards.’

Were they trying to take the money I earned after nearly dying?

It was probably because I’m the one with the last number, so I was the easiest target.

"Come on, come on. We'll only charge you half the cost of the water. Just half. Only half." 

"Wow. How incredibly generous of you." 

“Well…”

No way.

I clasped my hands in my lap, deftly dodging the hands of the guys who were trying to snatch 500,000 won, half of their daily pay, from me.

Then, before they could even react, I hurriedly spoke while fumbling through my pocket myself. 

“H-here you go.”

“Oh.”

“Here...”

Then, pretending to be flustered, I pulled something out of my pocket...

A cylindrical object.

“...What the hell is that?”

“Water.”

A plastic bottle of mineral water.

The exact same kind of water those guys had poured over my head yesterday.

'I bought it from the snack bar in front of the cafeteria.' 

The two of them looked dumbfounded.

I held out the bottle with the most innocent expression I could manage. Then, as if suddenly realizing something, I twisted the cap open and poured half of the water over my bowl of rice. 

“Ah, I-I’ll just give you half.”

Yes, I made it bold on purpose.

"Look at this little shit."

“Hey pigeon… for fuck's sake. Huh? Do you seriously not understand the situation you're in?" 

The atmosphere turned hostile in an instant.

One of them raised his hand, putting quite a bit of force into it, as if he were about to slap me hard across the face.

The hand came at me with enough force and irritation to make it clear he meant to ruin someone's day. 

‘Okay.’

I deliberately hunched my body to guide his hand toward my upper arm.

The moment his hand roughly brushed against the armband... 

"Ah…\!"

Startled, I jerked my body forward and looked at him with concern.

“T-this... they said it was expensive, and that anyone who breaks it won’t get paid…”

“…\!”

“Th-They're over there… If they catch you, you'll be in serious trouble." 

I pointed to the security guard standing outside the cafeteria, as if on watch.

“...Fuck.”

His eyes darted back and forth between the armband and the security guard. After a moment, apparently having done the math, he withdrew the hand that had been reaching for my head. 

It was about avoiding situations where you have to pay a hefty price.

“Ugh, what you just did just doubled the price.”

“You don’t even know what you just did, do you? I’ll kindly let you know as soon as possible. So look forward to it.”

You were really trying to act tough...

I sighed inwardly as I watched the two guys return to their seats.

I was so annoyed that I could die…

But I’d got to do what I’d got to do.

'I have a feeling he’ll try to beat me up somewhere, like in a hallway, around tomorrow.'

We’d see tomorrow. If that was even possible.

“Ha...”

I finished eating the cold rice, carefully drank all the water the guys had left behind… and then stopped by the shabby snack bar outside the cafeteria again.

“Th-thank you for this…”

And given the current situation, I spent a fortune on “supplies” and returned to the break room at the far end of the hall that had been assigned to me.

"They've cleared away the body..."

Z993’s body was no longer there. But the wet spot left by the body still hadn’t been cleaned up.

‘Ugh...’

I laid back down on my mattress, the one furthest away from that one.

“Let’s just get some sleep for now.”

And so, in that place—which was still in a dreadfully poor condition—I was able to review my plan with a bit more energy than I had yesterday before going to sleep.

‘Phew.’

And how much time had passed?

“......”

I sensed something.

In the darkness of the shabby, cramped break room.

The area under the mattress I was lying on suddenly started to feel damp....

“.....”

Wait a minute.

I had a weird dream tonight.

Something like a faceless ghost was cackling as it grabbed my ankle, lifted me upside down, and dunked me in the water—and then a snake…

‘No way.’

No... I was sure I buried it in the dirt.

My legs went stiff with tension.

........

I opened my eyes.

Then, rolling my eyes, I looked down.

That’s how our eyes met.

*Ha—*

A bizarre face, gripping my foot and grinning so widely that it looked like its head was splitting in half.

“....\!\!”

Strands of hair cling to my calves.

On the face, where the eyes and nose have swollen and smudged together, only two round, glazed-over black eyes are visible.

A swollen hand slips and pulls at my foot. Cold, bumpy flesh squishes against my calf.

My cold sweat smelled of fish.

*I-i-i-na-ta-ae-jo-eun-mi-s-geu-ju* ²

A smiling face with burst flesh was getting closer and closer. I was soaked through with cold sweat.

My cold sweat smelled of fish.

A nightmare.

‘Bite your tongue...\!’

Wake up. Wake up from this dream, no matter what\!

My hands twitched as I struggled desperately to break free, trying to move my fingertips just as I do when waking from a sleep paralysis episode.

I was strained. No—water was rising from deep within the mattress, right beneath where the cold sweat had fallen. I could hear a splashing sound. The drowning man was trying to lift my legs upside down. Unable to struggle, I just...

.......

...stopped.

*Youuuu*

I barely realized it.

‘...He’s buried\!’

On the other side of the mattress, on the exposed dirt floor, the drowning man’s lower body was half-buried.

That’s why he couldn’t grab me and drag me to the water’s edge. He couldn’t pull me under the mattress.

‘Wait a sec....\!’

I quickly twitched my hand, trying to rekindle the sensation of waking from a dream. I had to get out of here fast...

.......

.......

And then I realized.

‘Ah.’

There was a small error in the diary of the deceased Z993.

It wasn’t a dream.

I’d mistakenly thought I’d seen it in a dream, but that wasn’t the case.

‘I wasn’t… asleep.’

It was real.

The ghost had truly appeared in the break room where he was sleeping.

That was it.

Just as if it were right in front of my eyes.

"....\!\!"

*Hehehehehehe*

I froze in a daze.

A ghost grabbed my foot and shook it. My body swayed. All kinds of curses and screams echoed inside my mouth, but I was frozen with fear—I couldn’t even get a word out.

Fortunately, it didn’t last long.

Uh...

I fainted right there, still wide awake… 

'...I guess it’s a relief.'

That morning, after waking up and heading to the workshop, I held back a sigh.

Think positively...I could do this... on my own.

'I did have a bit of a nightmare, but at least I got some sleep and recovered.'

Yeah. The next morning, when I woke up and saw the muddy water stains soaking the mattress and the mold on the wall, my hair stood on end, but thanks to the morning roll call, I was able to leave the site right away—how great is that?

It’s not like it’s going to kill me right away, so what a relaxing horror story this is... I even let myself run through that absurd “happiness circuit” for a moment.

Though, in reality, it was slowly draining the life out of me.

And in my current physical state, if I’d been left alone for just one more day, I might actually have died of a heart attack, but…

\[Z999, enter the isolation room.\]

‘Can’t I just not go?’

Of course, that was out of the question.

I held back my tears as I got ready to go in.

Still, I knew one thing: I had brought everything I’d “prepared” without a hitch.

‘All right…’

I ran my hand over what I’d tucked into my arms, then took a step forward.

Squeak.

The isolation room door opened, and once again, I found myself trapped alone in the room with the figure from the ghost story.
