# **CHAPTER 372**

In the dark, secluded alleyways of the city.

A passerby went through the gap, casting a long shadow behind the telephone pole.

"Keugh\!"

*Thud.* The man, who bumped into a utility pole, lifted his head.

"F\*ck…\!"

The smell of alcohol wafted from his mouth.

The man was drunk enough to make anyone want to avoid him on the street. He murmured curses to himself, eyes hazy.

"Money, money…Fuck, fuck this f..." 

*Thud, thud\!* 

He kicked the pole with his feet.

He was on the verge of losing his mind, desperate to vent his anger on someone vulnerable.

500 million.

The debt he incurred while trying to recoup the money he lost on a sports betting site with cryptocurrency spread beyond primary and secondary financial institutions and into the world of loan sharks.

But even when he posted things like “Is the Han River water temperature warm these days?” online, and even as he drowned his despair in alcohol...

Somewhere deep inside his head, he knew the gravity of the situation with a certain numbness.

It just didn’t feel real at all.

‘I’ve racked up this astronomical debt?’

‘I have to pay it back?’

‘Am I ruined?’

“Isn’t there some way out?”

“This… uh, this can’t be happening, ugh…”

The moment his bloated ego, mixed with despair and a hopeless cycle, seemed to fly away…

*Flash.*

A dry light stung his eyes.

“Uh…?”

The drunk man squinted.

Taking a closer look, the light from the streetlamp reflected off something stuck to the body of the utility pole.

It was...

“A flyer?”

It was a flyer.

A glossy paper advertisement with a tacky appearance, commonly called a “flyer.”

> **Job Seekers Wanted**
>
>**Urgent\!**
>
> **• Next Up: From Community College Straight to a Major Corporation\!**
{: .paper }

Black text on a yellow background.

Some of the letters were missing due to tears, but the context was clear.

A job placement ad.

And two massive words slammed into his money-starved mind.

Job.

...Major company.

Major company.

“…What the hell. Why would a major company…Ugh, huh? Do something like this? Huh? Why are they handing out flyers?” 

Even the man’s alcohol-fogged brain couldn’t make sense of it.

The drunkard, glancing at the final phrases at the bottom of the cheap flyer—“Crazy Benefits” and “Start Working Now\!”—was about to slam into a utility pole in a fit of rage again when…

He staggered.

“Whoa…”

He lost his balance and fell forward.

His flailing hand clenched the flyer tightly.

*Crunch*—the flyer crumpled in his hand... revealing the back of the paper.

“...?”

There was more writing, scrawled in black marker.

> *Daily pay upon application\!*
>
> *500,000 won in cash*
{: .paper }
500,000 won.

“...”

Not 50,000 won, not 5 million won—500,000 won\!

A strangely realistic, substantial amount.

An amount that actually felt believable.

Yet it was large enough to be squandered on nightlife, falling into the category of a relatively ‘large amount.’

That was what caught his eye.

Plus….

“Enough to score it big…?” It was money worth investing.

Hope burst forth in his mind like fireworks.

‘If I work hard for a month and, uh, get paid… if it really pays off—whether it’s crypto or gambling…This time, for sure\!’

 Thinking about ‘recouping the principal,’ the drunk felt hope swell up in his mind as he swallowed hard.

‘Isn’t it worth a shot, even if it’s a little illegal or risky?’

‘And isn’t that why everyone else called? Are there still spots left?’

He got anxious.

“Where…?”

‘Where am I supposed to call?’

In a fit of anger, the drunk man tore it off.

*Sssshhh.*

Then he pulled his smartphone from his pocket and dialed the number for that “big company job.”

*070, 9393… Ring-ring-ring-ring… Beep.*

“Hello. I heard you’re hiring. Wait, are you really hiring? I saw your flyer—500,000 won, right? Is that for real? In cash?”

.......

“…Am I interested? Well, I’m calling because I’m interested. Right now? You want me to start right now? Yeah, yeah. I can start right now. Where… ugh… should I go? Um… huh?”

“You want me to check the number again?”

“What do you mean? Huh?”

8……

“Why is there an extra digit here? Uh, that’s weird, uh, uuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuu—”

0……

“Come here.”

The smartphone fell onto the dark alleyway floor.

*The number you dialed does not exist. Please check the number and try again…*

*Ring-ring.*

Silence fell as the call ended.

The smartphone screen, which had been flickering on the ground, went dark.

.......

There was no one left in the alley.

In that spot, illuminated only by the light from the utility pole, the coating on the flyer reflected the light once more.

“Soleum-ssi.”

“………”

“Soleum-ssi\!”

Ah.

My head snapped up.

In the brightly lit office, my boss was standing next to my monitor, tapping it with his finger.

“Have you been feeling unwell lately?” And here comes the standard workplace platitude...\!

I reflexively put on a professional smile and replied.

“I’m sorry. Was I too slow in responding?”

“No, I’m not doing this to get an apology…”

I’m not really apologizing either.

“... I’m just saying this because I’m worried—you’re just staring at a blank monitor during your break.”

“...Haha.” Oh, so that’s what it was…

I laughed awkwardly and looked away from the monitor.

‘What is this… am I just feeling sleepy?’ I guess I had been spacing out a bit.

There were five minutes left before the end of lunch break.

The office, with the air conditioning running, was fairly quiet. Most of the employees had already returned to their desks.

Just like my boss right in front of me now.

“Your performance is great—why are you so worried about what others think, even during lunch break?”

As it turned out, he was actually interpreting my behavior fairly positively.

“Why don’t you look for something fun to do? Hey, Soleum-ssi, didn’t you used to read something like that? That….”

....Wait a second.

Hold on a second...

“I mean ghost stories.”

“......”

The moment I heard that word.

A strange chill ran down my spine.

A strange feeling—part trauma, part nostalgia.

An otherworldly sensation.

I...

“Yes.”

I managed to reply.

“Thank you. Haha, I guess I really did catch a cold from the AC... I must have been a little out of it from relaxing too much.”

“Yeah, yeah. Let’s take care of ourselves and get to work.”

“Yes.”

Let’s relax.

Being too tense feels awkward.

Well, it was okay if I looked awkward, but there was really no need for it, so I could just let my guard down...

“Huh?”

My boss glanced behind me.

Towards where my bag is.

“Oh, do you carry one of those around too, Soleum-ssi? It’s cute with all that fluff.”

“…Huh?”

“No, I mean the stuffed animal on your bag. It’s a rabbit, right?”

“...\!”

I turned my head in a start.

Where my bag was. There was…

“Oh, now that I look again, it’s a puppy.” A popular white puppy character was hanging there.

…Not a pink rabbit.

“…Yes. That’s right.”

*Zing.*

A tingling sensation spread through me, as if electric currents were coursing through my heart.

Relief. Or… a sense of emptiness.

“Did your girlfriend give it to you, by any chance?”

“No.”

“Come on, you don’t have a girlfriend?”

“…Yes.”

“.......”

“......."

“Sorry...”

“It’s okay....”

My boss offered some polite advice like, “Anyway, be careful not to catch a cold from the AC,” and disappeared.

Phew.

I let out a sigh and leaned back in my chair.

That conversation just reminded me of a KakaoTalk message I’d received

> **\- Hey, Kim Soleum, want a blind date?**

> **\- You should definitely take this, lol**

> **\- You didn’t actually develop a phobia of potential girlfriends because of “that incident,” did you?**
{: .textmsg }

**Huh? lolol**

“..Hah.”

**\- Get lost.**

I flung my smartphone onto the desk.

Then I looked up at the ceiling where the air conditioner was blowing, glanced outside the blinds where the summer sunlight was streaming in, and... looked back at my desktop screen.

July 7\.

A somehow familiar date was displayed there.

And I soon realized why that date feels familiar.

It was the day I went to the pop-up store.

At the same time…

It was also the day I returned home.

“Ah.”

I see.

Before I knew it, 365 days had passed.

The day I returned to the ‘Darkness Exploration Records’ pop-up store.

In other words… the day when I finally arrived home.

I slipped back into my normal routine surprisingly quickly.

My normal life, free of ghost stories.

Going to work, coming home, and spending time with my family on days off.

Over the past few decades, the only thing that changed was the transition from school to work, but my unchanging daily routine has once again created a peaceful rhythm.

Human complacency and resilience worked surprisingly well.

Before I knew it, being sucked into another world—into those ghost stories—began to feel like a dream or a figment of my imagination.

When I return to my parents’ home and fall asleep peacefully, or when I meet friends and acquaintances, laugh with them, and return home without a care in the world, it all feels even less real.

...It felt like a lie.

That I ever went to the world of Darkness Exploration Records.

That I worked for a suspicious pharmaceutical company that grants wishes and a supernatural government agency.

That I met the characters from that world as real people.

But…

“.......”

Sometimes, I find myself flinching.

\-Stay tuned\!

When I hear that line on a TV talk show.

When the subway announcements crackle, when I’m filling the bathtub, when I hear the jingle while grocery shopping at a big-box store, when I see an ad for a counseling program, and…

When I happen to come across Darkness Exploration Records.

**\[The Horrifying Truth About the “Cure-All” Developed by Seven Researchers at a Famous Pharmaceutical Company / Qterw-B-486\]**

“.......”

Friday night after work.

I briefly turned on my smartphone in bed, then quickly scrolled past the YouTube video that had popped up on the screen via the algorithm.

Because the sense of reality, which had been fading away, sent a sharp tingle up my spine.

“Phew.”

Of course, that wasn’t the only reason.

It’s because I can no longer treat this as just an interesting story to pass the time.

When I read about the lives and fates of people I’ve actually met, how could I possibly not feel that sense of “I can’t stand it”?

I don’t have the heart to enjoy Darkness Exploration Records the way I used to.

At the same time, even imagining that I might come to enjoy it again as a “wiki”... repulses me.

I felt like I shouldn’t let that happen.

“...”

One thought leads to another, my mind racing.

If the world of Darkness Exploration Records that I visited still exists...

Is that world still connected to this one?

If so, how much time has passed since I returned?

How is everyone doing…?

“Stop.” 

...Let’s stop this.

It’s pointless to dwell on it.

“I can’t find out anyway.” 

Even if I checked the Darkness Exploration Records wiki, I wouldn’t know.

Ever since I fell into that world, events have been unfolding differently from what’s on the wiki, like the butterfly effect. But…

‘What if that’s been reflected on the wiki too?’

Given that the wiki is updated in real time, doesn’t that mean I have no idea what’s happening right now?

If I check it now…

 “...”

No.

I mustn’t let myself be swept away by impulse and restlessness.

I’d finally made it home—I shouldn’t stir up trouble where there wasn’t any.

‘It’s just because it’s nighttime.’

Besides, these worries and thoughts that pop up late at night usually disappear by morning anyway.

“Don’t do this on a weekend night—please, just go to sleep...”

Fortunately, that worked.

I left the living room light on, tossed my smartphone onto the side table, and sank deep into bed.

And just like that, I drifted off to sleep.

And then...

I suddenly opened my eyes.

“...\!\!”

It was a dark, vast indoor space.

I was standing there. After closing time, with the lights turned off...

In the basement of a department store.

*Woo-oo-oo—*

Shops selling sportswear, designer brands, and character merchandise. Right in the middle of that department store basement—which would undoubtedly be modern and atmospheric if the lights were on— was an empty space.

*Woo-oo-oo—*

A place where the interior had been stripped away, exposing the concrete.

It felt eerily empty, like a vacant storefront with a “For Rent” sign posted on it—as if everything had been stripped away...

But there was one thing left.

The tattered sign at the entrance.

**\[Prophecy of the Apocalypse: Darkness Exploration Records\]**

“...”

Right.

I was standing right where the Darkness Exploration Records pop-up was opened and then taken down.

But I didn’t panic.

“It’s a dream.”

Right. This was a nightmare.

And it was a situation I had already been through several times.

For some time now, I’ve been having dreams where I wander through the demolished space in the basement of the department store where the Darkness Exploration Records pop-up used to be held...

The first time, I screamed, but from the second time on, I was fine.

Because I knew it was just a dream.

“It doesn’t really exist.”

Yeah.

Hey.

‘There’s already another pop-up going on here.’

I’ve actually visited the basement of this department store again. That space, packed with idol fans, showed no trace of Darkness Exploration Records.

‘So, a place like this doesn’t exist in reality.’ It’s all in my imagination.

It was just a place created by my subconscious.

I knew that.

But....

“...”

*Rrrrrrr...*

The moment it went quiet.

I heard it.

*Rrrrrrr…*

From deep inside the dismantled pop-up space, from somewhere out of sight, I heard a sound.

*Rrrrrrr…..*

The sound of something operating.

The sound of something rolling.

A sound that reminds me of something I’d done myself.

That’s right...

The prize roulette.

*Clack clack clack…*

*Click.*

Someone was spinning the roulette wheel.

A sound coming from a pitch-black corner, from inside this street where it can’t be seen.

All I could hear was the sound.

*Clack clack clack…*

*Click.*

No\!

‘I’m imagining it.’ I—I’m imagining this…I must have spun that roulette wheel in a daydream, ended up at a new employee orientation, and that’s definitely what caused this trauma.

I was fine during the day, and when I fell asleep, my subconscious created this. Isn’t it a relief that it’s a lucid dream? I mean, the fact that I can reason this out.

So I could rest easy.

This is all just a dream my mind created\!

But....

*Rrrrrrr…*

*Thud.*

“....”

...What if it’s not just my imagination?

*Thud.*

Caught between fear and curiosity, my head felt like it was about to explode.

‘No.’

I gasped for breath.

And then, finally…

Unable to hold back any longer, I turned my head and stared at that spot.

The darkness beyond the sign…

…

*Beep-beep-beep-beep-beep\!\!*

*Thud*. I rolled off the bed and fell to the floor.

“…Whoa\!\!”

Damn it.

I reached out and turned off the blaring smartphone alarm.

My whole body was drenched in cold sweat.

“Ugh…”

I could’ve slept with the alarm off this weekend, but like an idiot, I left it on…

But thanks to that, I woke up.

“This is driving me crazy.”

I sighed, got up, and sat on the bed.

Fortunately, my sense of reality returned quickly.

After all, this wasn’t some eerie space where the pop-up had been torn down, but the bedroom of my own apartment, bathed in sunlight.

“...Phew.”

It seemed like these nightmares were getting more intense.

‘I didn’t hear any roulette-like sounds.’ That was a first.

It’s exactly the kind of experience that would be circulating online under a title like *‘(True Story) I Had a Scary Dream’*...

And given that I barely escaped from that ghost story in my dream, it’s definitely plausible.

“Haha....”

I lowered my head and looked at the screen where the notifications had stopped.

That is, my phone—the one I could use to access the internet.

“...”

*Worries and thoughts that pop up late at night usually disappear by morning anyway.*

That was usually true.

But today, they didn’t disappear.

So…

‘It might be better to face it head-on right now.’

I swallowed hard and swiped through my smartphone screen.

I opened the internet browser and, for the first time in a very long while, typed a familiar keyword into the search bar.

Darkness Exploration Records.

The search results appeared almost immediately, with a website link at the very top.

My heart was pounding.

I closed my eyes tightly, then opened them, and I…

**\[Prophecy of the Apocalypse: Darkness Exploration Records\]**

I clicked the page link.

Soon, the website’s homepage, filled with familiar text, appeared…

.......

“Huh?”

I blinked.

On the blank white page, only two sentences appeared, to no avail.

“.......”

*Tap, tap—*no matter how many times I tapped the screen, the page didn’t respond.

Did the site itself disappear?

Leaving only this page behind?

“Wait... wait a minute.”

On the emergency community forums and social media platforms I hurriedly found, people who were reading the wiki were all screaming in panic.

**\- Does anyone have a backup?**

**\- Haha, it’s not like there’s any copyright issue, so of course there must be one, lol. Please, come out, please.**

What on earth was this?

..“It disappeared the very moment I clicked on it?” Well, you could say I’m just being overly self-conscious… but a chilling sense of foreboding kept creeping up my spine.

A sense of unease.

**\- That message is pretty meaningful, isn’t it? I believe it—this is an event, right?**

Was it a typo?

But that sense of unease kept clinging to my spine, making me feel uneasy.

Just like... when exploring a Darkness.

That sensation—when you’ve overlooked something in a ghost story, and your survival instinct makes a light flicker—came alive again.
